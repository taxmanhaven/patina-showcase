# One field per line, "name: value". Lines starting with # are ignored. Craft: patina/craft/headlines.md
product: what it is, in a stranger's words (not the brand's)
reader: who sees the line, and their awareness level (unaware, problem, solution, product, most)
place: hero | page title | title card | ad | testimonial | end card | button
speaker: brand | owner | customer | narrator
one thing: the single fact the reader must leave with, in plain words
tension: two things the reader wants that seem to fight (their evenings and the customer who rings at 9pm)
after: what is different in the reader's week once it works, in their words (this is where appeal lives)
proof: the true detail that backs it (a number, a page, a named object)
next: what the reader does after reading
